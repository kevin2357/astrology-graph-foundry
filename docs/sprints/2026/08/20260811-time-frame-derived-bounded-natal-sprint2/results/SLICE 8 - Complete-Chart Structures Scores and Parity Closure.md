## Complete-chart semantics

The bounded structural graph describes only the retained invariant subgraph.
Deterministic indexes, object/relationship counts, topology counts, and root-owner
lineage are available. They are not probability estimates or independent evidence
weights.

AGF does not derive aspect patterns, dispositorship networks, elemental/modal
balance, structural-strength scores, or canonical claims from raw minute samples or
the invariant subgraph. Current exact Natal does not expose reviewed canonical
contracts for the first three; bounded scores would misstate retained evidence; and
projection/authoring claims remain downstream-owned. Angular and house structure is
represented only by individually proven canonical topology.
