Venus knows what feels valuable, beautiful, desirable, and worth saying yes to. It should not be forced to approve arrangements that the rest of the chart finds convenient.

The recurring 2026 transits are not random. They show where the year is repeatedly asking for attention. The best response is neither panic nor denial. It is conscious participation.
