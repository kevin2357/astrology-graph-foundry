In a report, this should be read together with Neptune trine Venus and Pluto sextile Venus. The whole Venus complex is active. Relationship, affection, beauty, values, and pleasure are not peripheral topics. They are being reconfigured.

## Eclipse/Lunation Layer: What It Adds

The Q1 eclipse/lunation package is a limited sample, but it clearly demonstrates usefulness.

It does three things a transit-only report does not do as well:

1. It marks narrative punctuation points.
2. It identifies dates where a lunation directly contacts natal material.
3. It helps decide which days might be worth reverse-reading or using as chapter headers.

The standout examples:

- **January 3 Full Moon in Cancer**: square natal Saturn, trine natal Moon.
- **January 18 New Moon in Capricorn**: opposite natal nodes, sextile Uranus, square Mercury/Pluto.
- **February 17 Aquarius eclipse candidate**: square Uranus/Venus, trine Mercury, near Ascendant.
- **March 3 Virgo eclipse candidate**: opposite natal Moon, conjunct Vertex, square MC/IC.
- **March 19 New Moon in Pisces**: trine natal nodes/Uranus/Venus.

For CFANFF-style reverse reads, the February 17 and March 3 windows are especially promising. They combine lunation intensity, node proximity/eclipse candidacy, and natal contacts that map to visible identity, emotional activation, disruption, and relational/meaning themes.
